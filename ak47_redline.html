Questions About AK-47 | Redline

What wear conditions does AK-47 | Redline come in?

The AK-47 | Redline is available in the following wear conditions: Minimal Wear, Field-Tested, Well-Worn, Battle-Scarred. Each wear corresponds to a float value range: Factory New (0.00–0.07), Minimal Wear (0.07–0.15), Field-Tested (0.15–0.38), Well-Worn (0.38–0.45), Battle-Scarred (0.45–1.00).

Is there a StatTrak™ AK-47 | Redline?

Yes. The AK-47 | Redline has a StatTrak™ variant that counts confirmed kills. StatTrak™ versions are significantly rarer than standard drops and typically command higher prices on the Steam Community Market.

How do I get the AK-47 | Redline?

The AK-47 | Redline can be obtained from the following CS2 weapon case(s): Operation Phoenix Weapon Case. Each case requires one case item and one $2.49 key from the Steam Marketplace. It is also part of the The Phoenix Collection.

What rarity is the AK-47 | Redline?

The AK-47 | Redline is Classified rarity — a very rare tier with a case drop rate of approximately 3.2%. Rarity tiers in CS2 from common to rarest: Consumer Grade, Industrial Grade, Mil-Spec, Restricted, Classified, Covert, Extraordinary.
